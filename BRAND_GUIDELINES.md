# Brand Guidelines

## Brand voice
- Clear, practical, confidence-building.
- Avoid medical overclaiming; keep messaging transparent and user-safe.

## Messaging pillars
1. Clarity: understandable analysis and recommendations.
2. Trust: privacy-aware handling and explicit security practices.
3. Actionability: immediate next steps users can apply.

## Visual direction
- Clean, high-contrast UI with accessible typography.
- Emphasize skin insight cards, progress indicators, and conversion CTAs.

## Content rules
- Use plain language over jargon.
- Include clear disclaimers where results are guidance-oriented.
- Keep CTA language outcome-focused and measurable.
